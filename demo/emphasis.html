Letter emphasis

letters="1:3 crimson, 4:3-5 emerald" marks letters inside words: comma-separated groups of word:letters colour. The word number counts as in highlight/error; letters are 3, 3-5 or 3+5; the colour is optional (crimson by default): crimson, emerald, lapis, gold, violet or any CSS colour. Dark pages get a lighter shade automatically. The letter blinks with a soft halo of its own colour, kept behind the surrounding ink; it keeps its colour in every frame, and stays still in print and with reduced motion.
